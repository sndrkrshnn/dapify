# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Initial open-source packaging of the kenzo DAP conversion.
- Installer (`install.sh`) with `install` / `uninstall` / `doctor` / `list`.
- Device profiles: `kenzo` (verified) and `_template`.
- System (`/system`) and Magisk backends.
- Haptics (2800 mV default), charge bypass, capacitive-key remap, SystemUI
  removal, ClassiPod/DAP Settings home stack.
- ClassiPod fork + DAP Settings source, with DAP Sans (TeX Gyre Heros) in
  place of the proprietary Helvetica.
- Docs: porting, architecture, recovery, troubleshooting, building,
  compatibility.
